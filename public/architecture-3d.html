03 / DECISION POLICY

Three outcomes. No silent bypass.

DSG may continue authorized work. Missing permission pauses an in-plan step; unsupported requests fail closed.

ALLOW

Within the approved plan. The scoped step meets required authority, capability, constraints and evidence conditions.

WAITING_PERMISSION

Still within the plan. A required permission or capability has not been provisioned. Resume after authorization.

BLOCK

Out-of-plan or unsupported. Policy violations, missing authority or failed proof cannot be converted to a success claim.
